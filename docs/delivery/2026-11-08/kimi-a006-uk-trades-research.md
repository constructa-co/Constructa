## 1. Why this matters for Constructa

Constructa's positioning ("built for small teams", trades onboarding with trade preferences) needs a grounded view of which UK SME construction disciplines actually exist across domestic and commercial work, and what vocabulary those firms use. This draft maps the landscape so product copy, onboarding trade lists and future content can speak the buyer's language without inventing segments.

## 2. Market context (sourced, dated 2026-11-08)

- **SMEs dominate UK construction.** AMA Research, citing ONS data, reports ~353,000 construction companies with turnover under £10m and states 99.1% of construction & built-environment businesses are classed as small ([AMA Research / Barbour ABI, 2023](https://www.amaresearch.co.uk/small-businesses-in-the-construction-sector/)). Construction employs ~3.1m people, ~9% of the UK workforce (same source). *Unverified beyond the cited page — treat as directional, not a live figure.*
- **FMB** (Federation of Master Builders) describes itself as the largest UK construction trade association with **8,000+ members**, "the recognised voice of small and medium-sized (SME) construction firms" ([UK Parliament written evidence, FMB](https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/71466/html/)). FMB was established in 1941 to protect SME construction firms.
- **SME housebuilders are a pressured, shrinking segment.** Reporting on Homes for Scotland places Scottish SME housebuilders at a 20-year low ([Inside Housing](https://www.insidehousing.co.uk/home/rising-costs-see-number-of-scottish-sme-house-builders-fall-to-20-year-low-92431)); a separate piece cites an ~80% fall in SME builders since the late 1980s ([The Landsite, 2021 data](https://www.thelandsite.co.uk/articles/number-of-sme-builders-plummet-80-per-cent-since-late-80-s)). Government policy attention (SME Accelerator Loans, planning reform) confirms the segment matters politically ([GOV.UK, 28 May 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-backs-sme-builders-to-get-britain-building)).
- **Implication:** the SME long tail is real and policy-visible; small firms doing domestic work plus small commercial jobs are the natural Constructa audience. FMB/NFB-style "local builder" framing matches this.

## 3. Discipline taxonomy — domestic and commercial

Below is a working taxonomy assembled from trade-association framing, recruitment-market listings and homeowner trade directories. It is a **draft map, not a product schema** — any onboarding/taxonomy change needs its own source-grounded action.

### 3.1 General builders / small works contractors (cross-over segment)
- **General builder / building contractor** — small firms quoting and managing whole jobs; often multi-trade, "we manage the job so every tradesman…" is characteristic directory language ([Home Handbooks example](https://homehandbooks.co.uk/companies/walker-windows/)).
- **Renovation & refurbishment / extension specialist** — domestic extensions, loft conversions, listed/period renovation.
- **Fit-out & refurbishment contractor** — the commercial-side counterpart (office/retail refurbishment), listed as a distinct recruitment discipline ([Phoenix Gray](https://www.phoenixgrayrec.com/construction-recruitment-midlands)).

### 3.2 Groundworks & civils
- Groundworkers, drainage, concrete, hard landscaping — described as working "across housing, commercial, civil engineering, and general building" ([Keighley College / apprenticeship standard](https://keighleycollege.ac.uk/subject_area/digital-it/)).
- Plant operators, steel fixers, banksmen/slingers ([Team Labourer](https://labourer.agency/), [Togher Group](https://www.toghergroup.com/international-trades-labour)).

### 3.3 Structural trades (superstructure)
- Bricklayers/blocklayers, stonemasons; carpenters & joiners (first/second fix); roofers (tiling, slating, flat roofing); plasterers/renderers; scaffolders ([Phoenix Gray trades guide](https://www.phoenixgrayrec.com/construction-trades-explained)).

### 3.4 Building services (M&E / plumbing & heating)
- Electricians; plumbers & heating/gas engineers (domestic "gas/heating engineer" is the directory label, [Rated People popular trades](https://www.ratedpeople.com/)); HVAC/refrigeration; fire protection; BMS on commercial side ([Construction Roles disciplines](https://www.constructionroles.com/discovery-call/)).

### 3.5 Finishing trades
- Painters & decorators; tilers; flooring; kitchen fitters; bathroom fitters; plastering; glazing/windows — homeowner directories treat kitchen fitter, bathroom fitter, plasterer/renderer as top-level categories ([Checkatrade](https://www.checkatrade.com/), [Rated People](https://www.ratedpeople.com/)). Multi-element bathroom jobs (plumbing, tiling, electrics, waterproofing) are exactly the "one quote, whole job" general-builder scenario ([ServiceMatch cost guide](https://www.servicematch.co.uk/cost-guides/bathroom-fitting-cost/)).

### 3.6 Specialist / professional disciplines (commercial-led, sometimes SME consultancies)
- Quantity surveyors, estimators, contracts managers, project managers, site managers/agents, site engineers, planning engineers, H&S advisors ([Hays infrastructure role list](https://www.hays.co.uk/jobs/infrastructure), [UCAS/CIOB/RICS-accredited career lists](https://digital.ucas.com/coursedisplay/courses/85d8e4df-04f6-8f8d-f411-5aba4619772f?academicYearId=2026)).
- Civil/structural engineers; M&E engineers; Clerk of Works. CIOB and RICS are the chartered bodies used as credentials for these roles ([CIOB vs RICS overview](https://www.theccm.co.uk/ciob-vs-rics-which-one-suits-your-career-better/)).

## 4. Contractor vocabulary cheat-sheet (for copy/onboarding)

Terms UK SME contractors actually use, drawn from the sources above — useful so product copy doesn't sound imported:

- **Job** (not "project" at the small end) — "pricing jobs", "winning the job".
- **Quote / estimate** — domestic buyers say "quote"; QS-led commercial says "estimate/tender/bid". Constructa already uses both.
- **Programme** (commercial) vs **schedule of works** (domestic small works).
- **Subbie / subcontractor**; **labour-only vs supply-and-fix**; **day rate vs price work / fixed price**.
- **Main contractor vs principal contractor** (CDM role) on commercial jobs.
- **BoQ / bill of quantities** (QS world) vs **schedule of rates**; **variation / extra** for scope changes; **dayworks** for unpriced extras.
- **Stage payments / valuations** (commercial) vs **deposit + milestone payments** (domestic).
- **Retention**, **defects liability period / snagging**, **practical completion / handover**.
- **CIS (Construction Industry Scheme)** — tax withholding regime for contractors/subcontractors; any "for contractors" content should respect this vocabulary.
- **CSCS card** as baseline site credential; **gas-safe registered** for heating; **NICEIC/SELECT** for electricians; **FMB/NFB membership** as trust signals.
- **Won job / pipeline** — sales language small builders use ("work winning" already appears in the product).
