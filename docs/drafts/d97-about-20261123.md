# About Constructa

DRAFT — Kimi K2.8 Preview copy, corrected by R1; unpublished. Intended placement: about.

Constructa is built for small UK contractors who price jobs from their own knowledge: scope, quantities, labour, plant, materials and subcontract rates. It brings those details together while keeping the judgement with you.

Capture the brief and quantities, enter your own rates, include preliminaries, overheads, risk and profit, lay out the work on working days, and review the proposal before sharing it. Check the scope and exclusions as carefully as the total.

Constructa does not measure quantities from drawings or decide your rates and margins. A programme needs your review against the real site, people and suppliers. The work-winning journey centres on company, brief, estimate, programme and proposal; it does not promise later delivery tools are ready.

Access is currently limited to existing review accounts. The preview is being checked before release for live customer work. New signup, payment and enquiry collection remain off.
