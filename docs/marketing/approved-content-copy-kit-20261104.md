## Topic 2 — Check programme and proposal before sharing

**Contractor angle:** Dates and stages should be realistic; read the full on-screen proposal and draft PDF, and understand client response options are not acceptance.

**Source paths:**

- `src/components/marketing/product-review-guide.tsx` — steps 03 “Programme” and 04 “Proposal”; reviewable vs held lists; next-step links to `/login` and `/pricing`
- `src/components/marketing/phase-one.tsx` — journey stages 03–04; hero illustrative proposal labelled “Illustrative example” (not a live project); phase-one scope and “not ready for live customer work” FAQ
- `src/components/marketing/product-screenshots.tsx` — synthetic programme capture qualifiers (optional stages, synthetic entries, not a standard programme promise)

### LinkedIn draft

**Status: DRAFT · NOT APPROVED · NOT PUBLISHED**

A proposal is more than a price PDF—it is dates, stages and terms read together.

In the phase-one product review guide, the programme step asks you to add a target start date (or working days from Day 1), how long the job takes, and optional stages. You get a programme at a glance and either a finish date or a finish day when the start is not fixed. The check: dates, working days and stage order are realistic for this job.

The proposal step adds payment stages, how many days the price stays valid, and the terms for this proposal. You get an on-screen preview and a PDF marked as draft—downloading that PDF sends nothing to a client. The guide’s check: read the whole preview. Client response options ask for receipt or a non-binding intention to proceed; neither is acceptance.

Constructa’s marketing home shows an illustrative proposal layout to explain the idea—it is not a live project and not something to treat as your screen today. Separate example screen captures on the features page are synthetic, from an earlier interface with mocked persistence; they are not the current hosted interface.

This is a working phase-one review, not release for live customer jobs. If you have a review account, sign in and work through programme and proposal in order. Questions about preview access stay on the pricing page or contact route—not a promise of new sign-up from these posts.

Programme and proposal in the guide: https://www.constructa.co/features  
How preview access works: https://www.constructa.co/pricing
