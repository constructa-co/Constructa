# Contractor guide pilot

Editorial draft for A103. The linked Guides are AI-assisted draft guidance for existing review accounts and await expert review.

## Check the brief before you price the job

Before pricing a kitchen refit, confirm who clears the room, when the property is accessible and whether the client supplies any fittings. Put those answers in the brief. Add quantities and rates to the estimate, then write down any exclusions that could change the price. Check that the company profile contains the details you want to use in the proposal.

[Review your company profile, brief and price](https://www.constructa.co/guides/review-brief-and-price)

## Check what could move the finish date

Read the programme alongside the estimate. Check that the start date, working days and stage order fit the job. Downloading the draft PDF sends nothing. The client can acknowledge receipt or express an intention to proceed. Neither response accepts the proposal.

[Check programme and proposal before sharing](https://www.constructa.co/guides/programme-and-proposal-before-sharing)

## Pilot review

Ask a contractor to read each draft and identify one check they would use on their next quote. Have the editorial reviewer compare the wording with the linked Guides and verify the public URLs before approving publication. Once publication and measurement are approved, review visits to each guide and clicks from each guide to the cohort application page. Record application submissions separately once an approved destination can confirm receipt.
