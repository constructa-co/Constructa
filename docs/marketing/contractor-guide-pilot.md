# Contractor guide pilot

Editorial draft for A103.

## Check the brief before you price the job

Before pricing a kitchen refit, confirm who clears the room, when the property is accessible and whether the client supplies any fittings. Put those answers in the brief. Add quantities and rates to the estimate, then write down any exclusions that could change the price. Ask the client to confirm unresolved assumptions before you send the proposal.

[Review your company profile, brief and price](https://www.constructa.co/guides/review-brief-and-price)

## Check what could move the finish date

List the steps that depend on a delivery or a client decision. For a refurbishment, agree when the client must choose finishes and check the supplier's lead time before promising a start date. Compare the programme and the proposal side by side. Make sure they describe the same scope and finish date before you share the draft.

[Check programme and proposal before sharing](https://www.constructa.co/guides/programme-and-proposal-before-sharing)

## Pilot review

Ask a contractor to read each draft and identify one check they would use on their next quote. Have the editorial reviewer compare the wording with the linked Guides and verify the public URLs before approving publication. Once publication and measurement are approved, review search impressions, visits to each guide and visits from the guide to the cohort application page. Record application submissions separately once an approved destination can confirm receipt.
