One job record in Constructa — brief, estimate, programme and proposal tabs all read fields you save yourself.

Before you share a draft PDF, check three links in the chain:

1. **Brief → price** — Scope on the brief can inform how you describe lines on Estimating, but totals are still lines you enter and review.
2. **Detailed scope** — When you save a full scope on Estimating, that wording replaces brief work text on the proposal.
3. **Programme** — Stages and working days you save on Programme feed timing in the proposal preview.

The guide walks through what carries automatically versus what you still type. AI-assisted draft guidance for phase-one review accounts; expert review pending.
