## Opening

Constructa keeps one job record across brief, estimating, programme and proposal. The phase-one guides describe practical checks: what saved fields feed each surface, and what you still enter and verify yourself.

[How saved job data connects across the review journey](https://constructa-nu.vercel.app/guides/how-saved-job-data-connects) is explicit that estimate lines and programme stages are work you enter. Brief scope can inform wording; a detailed scope saved on Estimating overrides brief work text on the proposal.

## Three checks before sharing

1. **Scope** — Does the proposal narrative match the scope you priced? If you saved a full scope on Estimating, does it say what you intend?
2. **Totals** — Are quantities, rates and section totals the ones you intend to stand behind?
3. **Timing** — Do programme stages and working days match access and duration for this job?

## Closing

Read the proposal preview and draft PDF before sharing. Downloading a draft PDF sends nothing to the client. Client response options ask for receipt or a non-binding intention to proceed, and neither is acceptance.
