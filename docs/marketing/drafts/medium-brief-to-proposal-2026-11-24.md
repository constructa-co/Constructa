## Hook

UK contractors lose time re-entering the same job facts on spreadsheets, emails and Word proposals. Constructa keeps one job record; the question is what actually carries between tabs — and what you must still verify yourself.

## What the product does today

The phase-one guides describe a review journey: company profile, brief and price, programme, then proposal preview. Saved fields on each tab feed the next surfaces you open; nothing is sent to a client until you choose to share.

The newest guide, [How saved job data connects across the review journey](https://www.constructa.co/guides/how-saved-job-data-connects), is explicit that estimate lines and programme stages are work you enter. Brief scope can inform wording, and a detailed scope saved on Estimating overrides brief text on the proposal.

## Three checks worth doing on every quote

1. **Scope** — Does the proposal narrative match the scope you priced? If you saved a full scope on Estimating, does it say what you intend?
2. **Totals** — Are quantities, rates and section totals the ones you will stand behind? Margins stay hidden from client PDFs; the all-in line is what matters commercially.
3. **Timing** — Do programme stages and working days match what you told the client about access and duration?
