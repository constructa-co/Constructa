## Before you start

You can read the public website now. The signed-in checks below are a plan for the approved practice copy, **not instructions to change the live site today**. The practice copy is currently unhealthy and has no verified backup. Wait until the build team confirms the correct copy is healthy, its backup and recovery checks are complete, and the particular checks are permitted.

Use an existing review account and a clearly named test job with made-up details. Use made-up company and past-job entries on the practice copy, rather than changing your real business information. Do not send or publish a proposal, accept terms, upload real customer photos, change account settings or enter real customer information. Signup, taking payments and collecting enquiries remain off. A successful automated test is useful preparation; it does not prove that the live site behaves correctly.

If a page fails to load, shows the wrong account's details or reports an error, stop and tell the build team what happened. Do not keep pressing Save or create another copy of the job.
